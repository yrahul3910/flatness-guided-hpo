### Principled normalization audit

The parent authorized analysis of gauge/parameterization normalization and revised claims after the initial findings. Two new quantities were derived from explicit invariances and exact local curvature identities, then frozen before checkpoint evaluation. The original ratio remains unchanged in production and in all historical evidence. `sweep-results/research-audit-20261002/normalization-audit/normalization_protocol.json` has SHA256 `0f9364f9d435483dfc3cbe282483b65ceea1686094b6428da85a255acb57b44d`. Formulas, aggregation, transformations, and tolerances were not chosen by comparing validation results.

For a Keras head kernel W of shape D by K, class-centering is W_c = W - rowmean(W). It removes arbitrary common-class shifts of each output-weight row. It does not remove adjacent-layer rescaling of the activation/weight ratio: h -> h/s and W -> sW still changes that ratio by 1/s^2. The earlier counterexample W=(0.1,-0.1) is already centered, so this correction leaves the false 5.024773 versus 0.495033 inequality unchanged.

The separately named candidates are C1, the centered relative-head trace `mean_i[(1-||p_i||^2) sum_j h_ij^2 ||W_c[j,:]||^2]`, and C2, logit-scale (inverse-temperature) curvature `mean_i Var_(k~p_i)(z_c,ik)`, where z_c is the centered full logit including bias. The frozen artifact identifier for C2 remains `temperature_curvature`. C1 is exactly the trace of the final-kernel cross-entropy Hessian under local perturbations deltaW_j = ||W_c[j,:]|| deltaTheta_j, with activations and row scales fixed at the current model. Zero row scales make that coordinate map singular; no invertibility is claimed. C2 is exactly the second derivative of mean cross-entropy along logits alpha*z_c at alpha=1. It is not the second derivative with respect to ordinary temperature T=1/alpha, which additionally has a first-derivative term under the chain rule. Its meaning is output-confidence curvature, not the original parameter-space flatness.
