Retain the kernel-only Euclidean geometry: fixed penultimate features and bias, with fixed per-coordinate isotropic perturbation variance. Unlike the fixed-width KMNIST panel, the CIFAR prior varies head width, so fixed per-coordinate noise also varies total expected perturbation energy with head dimension. State this limitation; do not silently divide by width/parameter count or introduce a new normalized arm. Report endpoint classification error separately by dataset.
