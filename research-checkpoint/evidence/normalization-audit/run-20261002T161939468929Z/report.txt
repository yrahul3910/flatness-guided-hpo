C1 is the gauge-centered relative head trace: mean_i [(1-sum_k p_ik^2) * sum_j h_ij^2 * sum_k Wc_jk^2], where Wc centers each weight row across classes. C2 is logit-temperature curvature: mean_i Var_{k~p_i}(z_centered_ik), including the saved output bias in z=hW+b. Both are arithmetic means across the 1500 examples. The original comparator is the repository's RMS(p)*RMS(h)/RMS(W) maximum across fixed consecutive batches of 64, including the final 28-example batch. The unweighted exact head trace is retained only as an explanatory diagnostic.

What this establishes: C1 removes the tested common-logit gauge and positive neuron-rescaling dependence of the original score, but exact duplication still divides it by r. It therefore fails a natural representation-comparability requirement. C2 is invariant across this fixed prediction-preserving panel, consistent with its function of predicted probabilities. The original score can change by orders of magnitude while the model's predictions stay fixed. Raw head curvature also changes under rescaling and duplication, so changing coordinates does not by itself make a head-curvature diagnostic invalid; it limits claims of representation-independent comparability.

What this does not establish: neither candidate is a repaired lower bound for the original theorem, a full-network flatness measure, an early-selection predictor, or evidence of generalization. C2 has different scientific meaning: a label-independent output-confidence curvature. Uniform predictions give zero and saturated predictions approach zero, so C2 alone cannot distinguish confident correctness from confident errors. No metric was chosen, sign-flipped, correlated with validation outcomes, or substituted into historical results based on this audit.
