Question: reviewer 6ZG2 requested correlations across runs at each epoch against the same runs' final validation losses, with current validation loss as the competing predictor. Protocol: cached MNIST official training examples [0,1500) train and [1500,1875) validation. Normalize float32 images by (pixel/255 - 0.1307)/0.3081. Eight configurations combine learning rates {0.0001,0.0003,0.001,0.003} and weight decays {0.00001,0.001}. Initialization seeds {31,47}; all configurations within a seed share initial weights and per-epoch shuffle order. Repository MNIST model: 32 filters, kernel 2, same padding, two blocks, hardcoded 128 hidden units. Five epochs, batch size 64, Adam and repository cosine decay over 120 updates, no early stopping. CPU-only deterministic TensorFlow execution, two intra-op threads and one inter-op thread.

Measurements: after each epoch, evaluate the CURRENT trained model in inference mode. Over the full ordered training subset in 24 batches (23 of 64 and one of 28), record every batch's probability RMS, hidden-activation RMS, final-kernel RMS, p*h/W ratio, RMS h/W ratio, and literal Frobenius h/W ratio. Each scalar proxy is its batch maximum. The current validation loss uses categorical cross entropy of the current model's probabilities; accuracy uses argmax. No call to get_convexity and no fresh proxy-model training occurs.

Interpretation: positive rho is the direction consistent with choosing smaller proxy values to obtain smaller final loss. Current validation loss has higher rho at every measured epoch in both seeds. The epoch-1 proxy relationship changes sharply across initializations. From epoch 2 onward, proxy and learning-rate ranks have rho +0.9759 within both seeds. Learning rate largely separates these short-run outcomes; tiny weight-decay differences can determine ranks within each learning-rate pair. These are exploratory observations, not significance tests or evidence about a 100-epoch endpoint. Do not select a proxy sign, dataset, seed, or hyperparameter range from this pilot and present it as a preregistered final result.

RMS versus Frobenius note: h_rms/W_rms = sqrt(K/B) * ||h||_F/||W||_F for hidden activations B x D and head weights D x K. Here K=10 and B is 64 or 28. Both batch-max variants were retained because the partial batch changes that factor. Their configuration rankings happened to agree in this pilot. Neither ratio is being asserted to bound Hessian curvature.
