Design

Each block, 401 or 419, uses the same eight fixed configurations: learning rate in {0.0001, 0.0003, 0.001, 0.003}, crossed with weight decay in {0.00001, 0.001}. The CNN architecture is fixed at 32 filters, kernel size 2, same padding, two blocks, and a 128-unit hidden head. Inputs use float32 pixels/255; no augmentation is used. This is a fixed panel, not adaptive HPO or a sample from a declared full search prior.

Four arms shortlist two candidates: minimum original float32 batch-maximum RMS ratio; minimum corrected exact mean-CE head trace; minimum one-epoch tuning CE; and a seeded random shortlist. All arms share the same complete, finite-score candidate pool. All 16 probes qualified; all also passed the legacy RMS > 0.1 rule. Although that rule was logged rather than imposed, imposing it would not change this candidate pool. Failed slots would not have been replaced; there were no failed phases.

Initialization is keyed to block, candidate and phase, independent of arm, rank or attempt order. Each shortlisted candidate is retrained from fresh initialization, with candidate Adam settings, a 100-epoch cosine horizon, maximum 100 epochs, and the actual Keras patience-25 validation-CE callback. Its minimum tuning-CE weights are restored. Among the two restored finalists, each arm selects by highest tuning accuracy, then lowest tuning CE, then candidate ID. Shared finalists are trained once and reused. Checkpoint selection by CE is distinct from the primary heldout classification endpoint.

What the corrected quantity means

T = mean_i [(1 - ||p_i||_2^2) ||h_i||_2^2] is the exact sample-mean cross-entropy Hessian trace for output-kernel entries in ordinary Euclidean/Frobenius coordinates, with penultimate features and output bias fixed. Probabilities for this identity are reconstructed in float64 from the saved affine head and checked against inference probabilities. The original RMS arm retains its original float32 batch arithmetic, including the tail batch.

The local perturbation interpretation uses fixed per-coordinate isotropic covariance. No perturbation radius, noise level or metric was tuned or sampled in this pilot. The trace changes under compensating feature/kernel rescaling and neuron duplication, does not describe the full network Hessian, and does not guarantee accuracy or eventual post-optimization performance. A valid restricted head-sensitivity identity therefore does not itself repair the original RMS theorem or prove the fast-screening efficacy claim. This pilot supplies no empirical advantage for trace under the frozen choices.
